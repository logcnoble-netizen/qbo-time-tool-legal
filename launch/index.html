You're connected to QuickBooks

BulkAuto is now connected to your QuickBooks Online company. The app runs on your Windows computer, so there's nothing more to do on this website.

Next steps

  1. Open BulkAuto from your desktop or the Start menu.
  2. Check that the company name at the top of the app is the one you meant to connect.
  3. Start with the Health scan to see what needs cleaning up, or pick a task from the sidebar.

If you signed in from the app and were sent to a page asking you to copy an address, paste that address into the app to finish connecting.
