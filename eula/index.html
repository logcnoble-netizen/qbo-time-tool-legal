5.Your data and changes you make

The Software reads data from, and makes changes to, a QuickBooks Online company only when you tell it to, after showing you what it will do. Some of those changes, such as deletes and voids, are permanent in QuickBooks and cannot be undone there. You are responsible for:

  • reviewing the preview before you confirm a change, delete or upload;
  • having the permission of the company's owner (for example, your client) to make the changes you make;
  • keeping your own backups and accounting records, including any reports you need before changing or deleting data;
  • checking that changes are appropriate for the company's accounting, payroll, billing, tax and record-keeping obligations; and
  • keeping the computer the Software runs on, and your Intuit sign-in details, secure.

Before it changes or deletes records, the Software saves a local backup, and most runs can be undone from its Activity list. Backups and undo are provided as a convenience and have limits: a deleted record that is re-created gets a new number in QuickBooks, a void cannot be reversed, and an undo leaves alone records changed in QuickBooks since the run. We do not guarantee that a backup will be complete or that data can be restored from it.

7.Subscriptions, fees and updates

Free trial. Each computer gets a free trial of five days from its first connection to a real (not sandbox) QuickBooks company, once per company. The trial needs no payment details and ends by itself; nothing is charged unless you subscribe. Sandbox (test) companies are always free.

Subscriptions. After the trial, using the Software with real companies needs an active subscription. Subscriptions are per bookkeeper ("seat"), billed monthly or yearly at the price shown at checkout before you pay. Each seat may be used on up to two computers. The Standard plan covers up to three client QuickBooks companies per seat in any 30 days; the Firm plan has no company limit. The Software checks these limits when it connects to a company.

QuickBooks reads. Intuit limits how much data apps like the Software may read from QuickBooks each month, across all of their users. So each plan includes a number of reads a month per seat (shown in the Software, in Settings), and a trial includes a number per computer. For now this is a guide: the Software warns you as you near it and doesn't stop working when you pass it. We may change the allowances, or ask a customer who regularly reads far more than the plan includes to move to a plan that fits, with notice.

Who you pay. Subscriptions are sold through Stripe's Managed Payments service. The Stripe affiliate named at checkout is the merchant of record for your purchase: it takes the payment, charges and pays any sales tax, VAT or GST, issues receipts and handles payment questions, under its own terms shown at checkout. We never receive your card or bank details.

Automatic renewal. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, and the price then in effect is charged to your payment method, until you cancel. We will tell you about a price change at least 30 days before the renewal it applies to.

Canceling and refunds. You can cancel at any time in the Software (Settings → Subscription → Manage billing) or by emailing us. Canceling stops the next renewal; the subscription keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. Payments are not refunded for partial periods, except where the law requires it or the merchant of record's terms provide for it.

Failed payments. If a renewal payment fails, the Software keeps working for a short grace period (currently three days) while payment is retried. After that it stops working with real companies until the subscription is paid or renewed. Your settings, backups and history stay on your computer.

We may release updates, fixes or new versions, and this Agreement applies to them unless they come with a different agreement. Versions of the Software that check for new versions ask you before installing one, and the check can be turned off in Settings. We have no obligation to provide updates or support.
