Getting started

What does BulkAuto do?

It cleans up QuickBooks Online in bulk: deleting or voiding transactions, reclassifying lines, removing duplicates, clearing Undeposited Funds, applying credits, writing off small balances, deleting old unbilled time, making unused names inactive and more. A read-only Health scan finds what needs attention first, and each finding opens the screen that fixes it.

Which QuickBooks does it work with?

QuickBooks Online only, not QuickBooks Desktop. It works with any QuickBooks Online company you can sign in to and connect apps to: your own, or a client's.

Does it run on a Mac?

Not yet. BulkAuto runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

How do I connect a company?

Open BulkAuto and click Connect to QuickBooks. Your browser opens Intuit's own sign-in page; choose the company and approve access. To add more companies, use Connect another company in the company menu at the top. BulkAuto never sees your Intuit password.

Can I try it on test data first?

Yes. Intuit's test companies (sandboxes) are always free in BulkAuto. In the app, Settings → Company switches between your real companies and a test company.

Safety and your data

What if a change goes wrong?

Every change shows a preview first and asks you to confirm. BulkAuto saves a backup of every record before changing it, and History undoes any run. History also exports a change log you can give your client.

Will it change closed periods?

No. Transactions in a period your QuickBooks closing date protects are left alone, and BulkAuto marks the ones it can't change.

Where does my QuickBooks data go?

Only between your computer and Intuit. Backups stay on your computer. The license check sends how many QuickBooks reads you used that month, never your QuickBooks data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect a company?

In BulkAuto, open the company menu and choose Disconnect this company from BulkAuto. You can also disconnect it in QuickBooks Online, under its connected apps. Your backups and history stay on your computer.

Plans and billing

How much does it cost?

A 5-day free trial with every feature, then Standard at $49.99 a month per bookkeeper (up to 3 client companies) or Firm at $99.99 a month per bookkeeper (unlimited companies). Paying yearly saves about 18%. See Pricing.

How do I subscribe or cancel?

In the app, under Settings → Subscription. A canceled plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

What are "QuickBooks reads"?

Each time BulkAuto loads records from QuickBooks counts as reads. Standard includes 20,000 a month per bookkeeper and Firm 60,000. The allowance is a guide: nothing stops when you reach it. Settings → QuickBooks usage shows this month's count.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, on 2 computers per bookkeeper. Settings → Subscription gives you a code to add the second one.

Problems

Connect to QuickBooks keeps connecting the same company

Your browser remembers the Intuit login you used last. Sign out at accounts.intuit.com, then connect again and sign in with the login for the company you want. More in Troubleshooting.

"The connection to this company has ended"

The company was disconnected, or BulkAuto wasn't used with it for 100 days. Click Connect to QuickBooks and sign in again; your backups and history are kept.

How do I get updates?

BulkAuto checks for a new version when it opens and about once a day, and asks before downloading it. It installs when BulkAuto restarts. Settings → Updates shows your version and has Check now.

Something else isn't working

Ask on the community board with your BulkAuto version and what you expected, without real names or amounts. Or email support@getbulkauto.com; in the app, Help → Contact makes a support file you can attach.
