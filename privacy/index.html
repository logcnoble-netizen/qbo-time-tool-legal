Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026Applies to: QBO Time Tool for Windows

In short

  • QBO Time Tool runs on your computer. We don't operate servers that receive your QuickBooks data.
  • Your QuickBooks data goes only between your computer and Intuit, over encrypted connections.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share your information.
  • Everything the app stores stays in your Windows user folder, and you can delete it at any time.

1.Who this covers

This policy explains how QBO Time Tool (the "app") handles information, and is provided by the individual developer of the app ("we," "us," or "our"). It applies to the desktop app and to this web page. It does not cover Intuit's services. Intuit's handling of your data is described in the Intuit Global Privacy Statement.

2.Information the app handles

The app handles only what it needs to do the work you ask for:

InformationExamplesSource
Intuit app credentialsClient ID and Client Secret for your Intuit developer appYou enter them in Settings

We do not collect your Intuit password. You sign in on Intuit's own pages, and the app receives only the tokens Intuit issues.

3.How it is used

The app uses this information only to connect to your QuickBooks company and do what you direct: loading, filtering and reviewing time entries, deleting the entries you confirm, creating the entries you upload, and saving backups and result logs. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4.Where it goes

The app sends requests directly from your computer to Intuit over HTTPS, including Intuit's sign-in pages (appcenter.intuit.com), its token service (oauth.platform.intuit.com) and the QuickBooks Online API (quickbooks.api.intuit.com, or the sandbox equivalent). Inside your computer, the app's window talks to its own built-in service, which only accepts connections from that same computer.

No information is sent to us or to any other third party. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to include in your message and use it only to reply.

5.Storage and retention

The app stores your settings, connection details, backups and logs in your Windows user profile, at %APPDATA%\QBO Time Tool. You can open this folder from the app (File → Open data folder). The data stays there until you delete it:

  • Disconnect in the app removes the saved connection tokens for the current company.
  • Deleting the folder removes all settings, tokens, backups and logs.
  • Uninstalling the app leaves this folder in place, so backups are not lost by accident. Delete it yourself if you no longer need it.

Data in your QuickBooks company is kept by Intuit under your agreement with Intuit.

6.Security

All communication with Intuit uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access tokens expire after about an hour and are refreshed automatically. Stored files are protected by your Windows account's file permissions, but the app does not add its own encryption to them. Protect them by using a Windows account password, keeping your computer updated and using full-disk encryption (such as BitLocker). Only install the app on computers you trust. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7.Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your information to anyone, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. Because the app does not send your data to us, we do not hold your QuickBooks data or credentials and cannot disclose them.

8.Cookies and tracking

The app does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers or crash reporting. Its built-in browser keeps ordinary local storage and cache files in the data folder so the app can run. Intuit's sign-in pages may set their own cookies, which Intuit's privacy statement covers. This web page adds no cookies or analytics of its own. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's standard request information, and the service hosting the page may set cookies under its own policy.

9.Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access. You can disconnect the app in QuickBooks Online from the apps you've connected to your company, and Disconnect it in the app. After that, the app can no longer reach your company.
  • Delete local data. Delete %APPDATA%\QBO Time Tool at any time.
  • Privacy laws. Depending on where you live (for example, under the Colorado Privacy Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move personal data, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. Because we do not receive or hold the data the app processes, requests about QuickBooks data should go to whoever controls that QuickBooks company, or to Intuit. For anything we do hold, such as an email you sent us, contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

10.Employee and customer data

Time entries can include personal information about employees, contractors and customers, such as names, hours worked and work descriptions. If you use the app with that data, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to process it and for any notices your employees or customers are owed. The app processes it only on your computer and only as described in this policy.

11.Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12.Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the app gains new features. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change materially affects how the app handles your information, we will note it on this page before the change applies to new versions of the app.
