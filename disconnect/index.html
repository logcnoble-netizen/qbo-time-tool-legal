Disconnected

QBO Time Tool is disconnected

QBO Time Tool no longer has access to your QuickBooks Online company. It can't read or change any of the company's data until someone connects it again.

Remove the saved data from your computer

Disconnecting in QuickBooks doesn't delete anything stored on your computer. To remove it:

  1. Close QBO Time Tool.
  2. Press Windows + R, type %APPDATA%\QBO Time Tool and press Enter.
  3. Delete tokens.sandbox.json and tokens.production.json to remove the saved connection. Delete the whole folder to remove settings and backups too. Backups are your only copy of entries the app deleted, so keep them if you might need them.

You can also uninstall the app from Windows Settings → Apps. To use the app again later, see Connect QBO Time Tool.
