Download

BulkAuto for Windows

Bulk cleanup for QuickBooks Online, with a preview, a backup and an undo for every change.

Download BulkAuto Windows 10 or 11, about 110 MB. The latest version, BulkAuto-Setup.exe.

The installer is signed by its publisher, Logan Noble.

Install

  1. Run the installer.

    Open the BulkAuto-Setup file you downloaded. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Windows can show this while a new publisher's signature builds its reputation.

  2. Accept the License Agreement.

    The installer shows the End-User License Agreement. Accept it, choose where to install, and finish. BulkAuto is added to the desktop and the Start menu.

  3. Click Connect to QuickBooks.

    Open BulkAuto and click Connect to QuickBooks. Your browser opens Intuit's sign-in page: choose the company and approve access, and the app connects by itself.

Updates

Updates install from inside the app. BulkAuto checks for a new version when it opens and about once a day, sending only its version number. It asks before downloading, checks the update is signed by its publisher, and installs it when the app restarts, never during a run. Settings → Updates turns the check off and has Check now.

Trial and plans

Sandbox (test) companies are always free. For a real (production) company, a 5-day free trial starts the first time the app connects one, with no payment details needed. Start a free trial from the app; choose a plan in Settings → Subscription.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • A QuickBooks Online company, and permission to connect apps to it.
  • Your QuickBooks data goes only between your computer and Intuit. See the Privacy Policy.
