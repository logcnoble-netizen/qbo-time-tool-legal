Disconnected

BulkAuto is disconnected

BulkAuto no longer has access to your QuickBooks Online company. It can't read or change any of the company's data until someone connects it again.

Remove the saved data from your computer

Disconnecting in QuickBooks doesn't delete anything stored on your computer. To remove it:

  1. Close BulkAuto.
  2. Press Windows + R, type %APPDATA%\BulkAuto and press Enter. (If you had the app before it was renamed from QBO Time Tool, type %APPDATA%\QBO Time Tool instead.)
  3. Delete tokens.sandbox.json, tokens.production.json and the companies folder to remove the saved connections. Delete the whole folder to remove settings and backups too. Backups are your only copy of records the app deleted or changed, so keep them if you might need them.

You can also uninstall the app from Windows Settings → Apps. To use the app again later, see Connect BulkAuto.
