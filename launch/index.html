Connected

You're connected to QuickBooks

QBO Time Tool is now connected to your QuickBooks Online company. The app runs on your Windows computer, so there's nothing more to do on this website.

Next steps

  1. Open QBO Time Tool from your desktop or the Start menu.
  2. Check that the company name at the top of the app is the one you meant to connect.
  3. On the Delete tab, choose a date range and click Load entries. On the Upload tab, choose a spreadsheet of time entries to add.

If you signed in from the app and were sent to a page asking you to copy an address, paste that address into the app to finish connecting.
