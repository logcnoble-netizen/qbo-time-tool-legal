BulkAuto for Windows

BulkAuto cleans up QuickBooks Online companies in bulk: delete or void transactions, upload records from spreadsheets, change fields, apply payments and credits, write off small balances, group deposits, make names inactive and more. Every change shows what it will do first, asks you to confirm, saves a backup, and can be undone from History.

Download BulkAuto Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Signed by its publisher, Logan Noble. It updates itself, and asks before installing anything.

Free for 5 days

Each computer gets a free trial of five days from its first connection to a real QuickBooks company. No payment details are needed, and nothing is charged unless you subscribe. Sandbox (test) companies are always free.

Plans

Per bookkeeper, each on up to two computers. Subscribe in the app, under Settings → Subscription; payment is handled by Stripe.

Standard

$49.99 a month or $490 a year, per bookkeeper. Up to 3 client companies and 20,000 QuickBooks reads each a month.

Cancel any time in the app (Settings → Subscription → Manage billing); it keeps working to the end of the period you've paid for. See the License Agreement for the full terms.

What you need

  • A QuickBooks Online company you're allowed to change, and its Intuit sign-in. BulkAuto connects with Sign in with Intuit and never sees your password.
