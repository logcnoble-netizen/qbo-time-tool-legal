Disconnected

BulkAuto is disconnected from QuickBooks

The connection between BulkAuto and your QuickBooks Online company has ended. BulkAuto can no longer read or change that company's data until someone connects it again.

Backups and history on your computer are kept. Activity still lists the changes the app made, and they can be undone after you reconnect.

Reconnect

  1. Open BulkAuto from the Start menu or your desktop.
  2. Click Connect to QuickBooks.

    Or, if the app is working on another company, open the company menu at the top and choose Connect another company.

  3. Approve access on Intuit's sign-in page.

    Choose the company and approve. The app connects by itself. You need to be an admin of the company, or allowed to connect apps to it.

Removing BulkAuto completely

To remove the app, uninstall it from Windows Settings → Apps. Uninstalling leaves the data folder, with your backups and history, in place. The Privacy Policy explains where it is and how to delete it.
