Connect or reconnect

Connect BulkAuto to QuickBooks

BulkAuto connects to QuickBooks Online from the app on your Windows computer. To connect for the first time, or to reconnect after disconnecting or after your connection expires:

  1. Open BulkAuto on your computer.
  2. To connect a different company, click the company name at the top right and choose Connect another company. The ones you connected before stay in that menu.
  3. Click Sign in with Intuit, sign in to your Intuit account, choose the QuickBooks company and approve access.
  4. If Intuit sends you to a page with a long address (only when you use your own Intuit app keys), copy the whole address from the browser bar and paste it into the app.

You need to be an admin of the QuickBooks company, or have permission to connect apps to it. You'll need to reconnect if the app goes unused for about 100 days, or if someone disconnects it in QuickBooks.

Don't have the app yet? Ask the person who shared it with you for the installer.
