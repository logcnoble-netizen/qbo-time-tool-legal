Open BulkAuto

BulkAuto runs on your computer

BulkAuto is a Windows desktop app, not a website. You use it in its own window on your computer, and it connects to QuickBooks Online from there.

Open the app

  1. Open the Start menu and choose BulkAuto.

    You can also double-click the BulkAuto shortcut on your desktop.

  2. Check the company.

    The company menu at the top of the app shows the QuickBooks Online company you're working on. To use another one, choose Connect another company in that menu.

  3. Not connected yet?

    Click Connect to QuickBooks in the app and approve access on Intuit's sign-in page.

Not installed?

Download BulkAuto for Windows 10 or 11 and run the installer.
