Connect from the BulkAuto app

BulkAuto connects to QuickBooks Online from the app on your Windows computer. There's nothing to do on this page.

  1. Open BulkAuto from the Start menu or your desktop.
  2. Click Connect to QuickBooks, or choose Connect another company in the company menu.
  3. Approve access on Intuit's sign-in page. The app connects by itself.
